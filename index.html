JARVIS

JARVIS is a personal voice assistant that runs on your own computer. Say "Hey Jarvis" and it can read and answer your email, work with your Google Docs and Sheets, and help run your social media.

How it uses your Google account

When you connect Google, JARVIS can read and send mail in Gmail, read your Drive files, create and edit Docs and Sheets, and read your Google Analytics numbers. It only does this on your own computer, for you. Anything that sends or changes something asks you first.

Privacy policy

JARVIS runs locally. There is no JARVIS server and no account to create.
