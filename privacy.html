Privacy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Where your data goes

JARVIS runs entirely on your own computer. There is no JARVIS server. We do not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

Google data JARVIS uses

  • Gmail: to read, sort, draft and send email when you ask.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: to find and read your files, and to create or edit documents and sheets when you ask.
  • Google Analytics: read only, to show your website numbers.

This data is fetched directly from Google to your computer. To answer you, JARVIS may send parts of it to the AI model you have set up (such as Anthropic's Claude), under that provider's own terms. It is never used for advertising and never sold.

TikTok data JARVIS uses

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): your TikTok username, to show which account is connected.
  • Video posting (video.publish): to upload and publish the videos you have approved, with their captions, to your own TikTok account.

JARVIS does not read your followers, messages, comments or other users' data on TikTok. Videos and captions go directly from your computer to TikTok.

How sign-ins are stored

Google and TikTok sign-in tokens and mail passwords are encrypted with Windows' own protection (DPAPI) and saved only on your computer. They can't be read on any other machine.

Removing access

Remove an account in JARVIS under Settings → Connections, and revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions (TikTok).

Google API Services User Data Policy

JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google or TikTok sign-in screen for this app.
